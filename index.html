Privacy Notice

This community-service board collects information that parents or authorized adults voluntarily provide when signing up for an event.

What we collect

  • Parent/guardian name
  • Child/participant name
  • Email address
  • Optional phone number

Why we collect it

The information is used to organize service projects, maintain attendance records, and communicate about the event.

Who can see it

Participant details are kept in a private administrative Google Sheet and are not displayed on the public event board. Public visitors only see the number of confirmed participants.

Changes and cancellation

Participants can use their confirmation code and signup email to cancel an active signup.

If you have questions about information submitted through this board, contact the person operating the community-service program.
